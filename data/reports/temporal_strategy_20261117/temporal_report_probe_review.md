Evidence:
1. recorded_memory_dependence creates one final GRU model and hidden=zeros once per checkpoint. Each saved observation advances the carried hidden once chronologically. The independent cleared call always receives fresh zeros and discards its returned hidden, so it cannot contaminate the carried branch. Initial episode_start need not be supplied because all states begin exactly at zeros and the saved trace is one episode. Replay action is compared to saved greedy action at every timestep, with mismatch raising before report publication.
2. Compared distribution.logits are Categorical's normalized logits (log probabilities), and only mask-legal entries are reduced. Thus maximum_legal_normalized_logit_difference is invariant to additive raw-score offsets and excludes sentinel illegal scores. Forced-single-choice rows legitimately contribute zero difference and remain counted separately in multiple_legal_choices. No claim that different logits require different actions.
3. recorded_learning_change initializes separate final_hidden and initial_hidden and advances each with its own model for the SAME saved chronological observation sequence. It does not mix recurrent states from different parameter versions. KL(before,after) is the directional full legal categorical KL(initial||final); both have identical legal masks, finite probabilities on legal actions and identical zero mass on illegal ones. Result is trajectory-conditional policy change, not initial-vs-final return or paired gameplay.
4. First-case identity uses registration first seed/style/side=(51001,capture,0), identical to evaluator itertools.product ordering. Evaluator NPZ filename is exactly label_seed{seed}_{style}_side{side}.npz with keys state,candidates,mask,action; helpers match it. Only original learned checkpoint labels occur in evaluation.checkpoints, so helper paths do not accidentally load *_reset traces.
